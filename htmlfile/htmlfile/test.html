Our History

Aahar Seva was a idea of two university students with the mission to provide nutritious meals to underprivileged children and families in urban slums. The organization started with a small team of volunteers who were passionate about making a difference in their community.

Our founders

Aahar Seva was founded by Rajat Agarwal along with Mukund Jha and a group of like-minded students who believed that no meal should ever go to waste while someone goes hungry. What began as a simple initiative on campus has now grown into a platform that connects restaurants, NGOs, and volunteers across the city.

Why We Started

Every day, restaurants and events end up with large amounts of surplus food while countless people struggle to find their next meal. Aahar Seva was created to build a bridge between these two worlds. Whenever food remains in a restaurant, the platform alerts nearby NGOs and community kitchens so that the food can be collected and shared quickly. This not only reduces food wastage but also ensures that the needy receive fresh, nutritious meals on time.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to eliminate hunger by redistributing surplus food to those in need. We envision a future where no child sleeps hungry and every meal finds a purpose. Through technology, partnerships, and compassion, we aim to create a sustainable food-sharing ecosystem.

Our Partners

We collaborate with restaurants, hotels, event organizers, and NGOs to ensure food reaches the right hands at the right time.
